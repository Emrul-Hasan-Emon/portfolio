Section 01

About Me

I'm a Software Engineer at Grameen HealthTech Limited, where I build the backbone of Shukhee — a healthcare e-commerce platform. My day-to-day spans database design and microservices across its Admin, Seller, Customer, and Rider domains, an OpenSearch-powered product discovery service that cut search time by 3x, and the payment, coupon, and login systems that keep the platform running smoothly at scale.

Before joining Grameen HealthTech, I spent close to two years at Kaz Software as an Associate Software Engineer, where I helped lead the Tax Research Platform's migration from Endeca to Elasticsearch and rebuilt its document-comparison tooling for 3x faster page loads — my first deep lesson in shipping a large-scale migration without breaking production.

I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering from IUBAT (CGPA 3.93), where I also trained students in C/C++ and competitive programming and represented my university at the ICPC Asia regional and continent-final level. I'm a Codeforces Expert with 2000+ problems solved, and I write about Go, system design, and web architecture on Medium.

Outside of engineering, I'm an extrovert at heart — I love traveling to new places, meeting new people, and hearing their stories. Whether it's a programming contest hall or a city I've never visited before, I'm always up for striking up a conversation.
